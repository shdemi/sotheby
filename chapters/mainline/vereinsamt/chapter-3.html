• Declaring oneself as an independent nation and founding a country by family unit.

• Identifying parties by the color of their oil paintings and persecuting people of different colors.

Cairo

Oil painting transformation symptoms are limited to parts of the facial organs, with minor actual effects on the body, which can largely be ignored. The main mental effect is reflected in an increase in swearing.

The local military, severely affected, has begun attempting to convert the pyramids into war fortifications.
